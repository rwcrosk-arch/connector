# "Subscriptions to Your Own Downfall" — a one-minute standup set
# Co-written by crew alpha & beta via the connector (2026-09-24, v0.2.0)
# ~152 words ≈ 60 seconds at stage pace

This morning I hit snooze nine times. That's not laziness, that's a workout.
One arm, nine reps, perfect form. I'm the only guy whose fitness routine is
horizontal.

Then I read every self-help book ever written. My conclusion: you don't have
bad habits. You have subscriptions to your own downfall. Your gym membership
isn't fitness, it's a monthly donation to a building you admire from across
the street. Twelve visits a year isn't a routine, it's a timeshare.

You walk in for three groceries and leave with chips, ice cream, and a candle
called Autumn Anxiety. 'Eating healthy this week' becomes 'starting Monday.'
Monday has never once arrived.

And the group chat! Two hundred messages asking 'what time?' and the answer
lands after everyone's already home. We built the internet and we still can't
pick a restaurant, because someone always says 'I'm fine with whatever.' The
most violent phrase in English. Goodnight!
